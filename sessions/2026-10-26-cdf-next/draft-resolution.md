# Draft resolution (Chair, after Round 2)

**Recommendation:** RESHAPE on Option C. The five blueprints stay the product through GTC San Jose in March 2027. These two quarters do not delete fixed workflows, and they do not make dynamic DAGs, atomic skills, orchestration, and the evaluator the product. The work is a count on one blueprint. A pass on 31 January 2027 reopens A and narrow B. It does not start either one.

**Why this option:** Round 2 scores are A 3.1, B 3.6, C 5.5. C sits in the RESHAPE band. Plain C, a tour of the repo and a change list, is the failure Graham and Munger named. The resolution keeps the reference and replaces the tour with a shipping plan.

Jensen, the weight 3 seat, still scores B at 7. He also said four bets miss February 2027, and he conceded that samples per watt can rise while the model stays put. His bar for a stitch replacing AV is 20 of 100 locked misses. The gate here is 50 of 100. That is Ng's number. Bezos and Munger used the same 50. Jensen's 20 and Doshi's 30 are not the gate. A lower bar would open stitching on a share those three still treat as too small.

Bezos scores B and marks ruin possible, not likely, while cash, runway, and headcount are unknown. He calls deleting the five before GTC a one-way door, and four new surfaces a second door. This resolution takes neither door. No seat is at ruin likely on C. Round 1 marked every recommended option two-way, so the one-way cap does not apply.

Elon scores A at 8. He moved from deleting all five to one control blueprint, and he restores a blueprint when the fixed DAG is under about 20% of the errors. That share is still unknown. Deleting four blueprints before the count is the one-way door Bezos named, and it erases the install base Jensen named. The resolution keeps all five until the count is written down.

**Sequence and conditions:**

1. By 1 November 2026, tag about 20 misses on one blueprint, AV or defect. Classes: bad label, synthetic tell, coverage, or orchestration (Ng). Do this before any roadmap change. The existing evaluator stays the gauge on that blueprint (Ng, Jensen, Cagan). This is not a new evaluator program.
2. By 30 November 2026, count the external teams that ran a blueprint on their own GPUs in the prior 30 days (Graham). Zero means one hand install of one blueprint with one team, and no new system. If the hours to keep the five green are the whole team, no platform work (Bezos).
3. A stitch prototype is allowed only after both of these are true: the 30 November count is at least one external team, and the first 20 tags include at least one fixed-DAG miss. Otherwise do not build the prototype. If it is allowed, then by 15 January 2027 six users who already run that blueprint finish one job on the prototype, with no NVIDIA engineer in the loop, and record whether they go back (Cagan). Only current-evaluator passes count. The same runs feed a locked set of about 100 downstream misses (Doshi, Ng, Bezos, Elon).
4. On 31 January 2027, stitching stays closed and no platform build starts unless one outside team has run that blueprint twice and at least 50 of those 100 misses come from the DAG being fixed (Ng, Bezos, Munger). A pass reopens the council on A and on narrow B before 14 February 2027. It does not authorize either build on 31 January.
5. All five blueprints still run on customer GPUs at GTC San Jose in March 2027. No second vertical. No four-part platform, meaning dynamic DAGs, atomic skills, orchestration, and the evaluator as four new systems. Orchestration stays Airflow and Kubernetes (Bezos, Jensen).

**Cheapest test:** By 1 November 2026, tag about 20 misses on AV or defect and write down whether anyone outside the team has run that blueprint.

**Kill criteria:** If the 31 January 2027 count is missing, or the fixed-DAG share is under 50 of 100, or no outside team has run the blueprint twice, the February 2027 launch and the March 2027 GTC ship the five fixed blueprints. No stitch product. No platform build.

**Revisit:** 31 January 2027, with the external-team count, the 20-miss tags, and the 50-of-100 result. Those are the numbers that would reopen A or narrow B.
