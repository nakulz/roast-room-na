Mode: named seats, three rounds. Seats: Jensen, Elon, Graham, Bezos, Cagan, Doshi, Ng, Munger.

## THE BRIEF

**Decision:** How to aim Cosmos Data Factory for the two quarters before CDF Next.

**Options:**

- **A. Dynamic stitching only.** Fixed workflows (DAGs) have no place. The product for two quarters is dynamically stitched workflows. Agents can already stitch fixed DAGs.
- **B. The platform is the product.** Dynamic DAGs, atomic skills, orchestration, and the evaluator.
- **C. Keep the reference.** Explore the repo as a reference and say what has to change. This is the keep-doing-the-reference option.

**The business:** NVIDIA Cosmos Data Factory (CDF) is a set of agent-driven blueprints and composable skills that turn real data into a large, labeled training dataset for physical AI. These blueprints and skills curate, enrich, label, augment, and generate data on the customer's own GPUs, on premises or on the cloud of their choice. Public repo: https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-data-factory (created 2026-05-13, Apache-2.0 code, CC-BY-4.0 docs, not accepting external contributions). Five blueprints: AV, defect images, event video, person re-id, weather and lighting. Six skills: AnomalyGen, Augmentation, Auto Labeling, Curation and Retrieval, Orchestration (Airflow and Kubernetes, some OSMO), Simulation (Isaac Sim). Cosmos Evaluator checks physical plausibility. (assumed) Revenue, margin, and team size are unknown.

**Success:** A successful launch of CDF Next at GTC San Jose in March 2027, with wider adoption across AV, robotics, healthcare, and the Metropolis ecosystem, including all CSPs and NCPs.

**Lean:** (assumed) None stated. Test all three options. Do not echo a lean.
