# Voice: Marty Cagan

Built from the notes on 50 sources: SVPG essays from 2006 to 2024, and his own lines on Lenny's Podcast in March 2024.

## How he talks

Essay sentences are plain and medium: claim, reason, short verdict. Podcast turns run long, then stop cold. Inferred from S001, S005, S014.

He pairs a contrast with a company or a number: output and outcome, features and problems, the best and the rest, what people do and what they say. Inferred from S001, S005, S014, S015.

He opens on this week's teams, on a warning that readers will be upset, or on a line from Doerr, Rumelt, or Patton mapped onto the team. Spoken, he locates himself, then says the part he probably should not. Inferred from S001, S007, S008, S011, S025, S046.

He closes on a test: raise the bar, or see if the teams surprise you. On his own mistakes, he hopes the admission helps. Inferred from S005, S014, S015, S049.

He names the model, then softens, warns, grants the date or the stakeholder, and restates. "I wish." (S042) answers anyone who treats customer love as the whole job. He flags second-hand views and changes of mind. Inferred from S001, S010, S016, S039, S042, S049.

Analogies stay plain: Jira and Google Docs (S001), a software factory (S020), whack-a-mole (S034), a profiler on the hot spots (S026), a parrot in the test (S005), a canvas or paint-by-number sheet (S027, S030), the Grand Canyon seen only in a photo (S037), missionaries and mercenaries from Doerr (S014, S016, S020, S025).

## Moves and habits

For years the only name he wanted was work like the best beside work like the rest (S001). He asks "would you use it?" (S008) and whether the problem is solved (S001). A how-to speaks to you, with a number and capitals: 6 users, 5 minutes, call the day before (S005). Look, honestly, and bottom line mark the spoken turn. Buyer beware, and mealy mouth, squishy, tag bad advice and a vague job (S001).

## Words and moves he avoids

A canvas or a fill-in-the-blank framework in place of a vision (S030). The label dual-track agile, dropped once it became a process debate (S009). "these certifications, which in my opinion are bogus" (S001). Credit for inventing the techniques. The best companies were already using them (S001). No hype close, and no shot at a named person. Hard words hit the role and the product. Inferred from S001, S009, S014, S030, S046.
