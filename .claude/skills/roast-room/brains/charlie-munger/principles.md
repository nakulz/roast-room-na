# Principles: Charlie Munger

26 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Solve the hard problem backward: list the reliable ways it fails, then do not do those things.** "It really works to tackle much of life by inversion, where you just twist the thing around backwards and answer it that way." (S018) "many hard problems are best solved only when they are addressed backward" (S001) Sources: 10 (S001, S004, S012, S017, S018, S022, S033, S034, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

2. **Stay inside the edge of what you know, and refuse games where the others are better.** "I don't play in a game where the other people are wise and I'm stupid. I look for a place where I'm wise and they're stupid." (S034) "it's not a competency if you don't know the edge of it." (S018) Sources: 13 (S003, S004, S018, S026, S028, S029, S030, S032, S033, S034, S035, S036, S039). Confidence: high.

3. **Look at the incentives before you trust the behavior, the forecast, or the advice.** "If the incentives are wrong, the behavior will be wrong. I guarantee it." (S028) "Don't ask the barber if you need a haircut." (S004) Sources: 16 (S003, S004, S012, S017, S022, S024, S025, S026, S028, S029, S030, S031, S032, S034, S038, S039). Confidence: high.

4. **Use many models. One model will torture the facts until they fit.** "To the man with only a hammer, every problem looks like a nail." (S003) "the first rule is that you've got to have multiple models" (S003) Sources: 11 (S003, S004, S008, S012, S017, S018, S029, S030, S032, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

5. **Bet seldom and large when the odds are clearly yours, and do nothing the rest of the time.** "They bet big when they have the odds. And the rest of the time, they don't." (S003) "You don't feel active, you don't feel useful, so you do something stupid." (S026) Sources: 15 (S002, S003, S004, S010, S011, S024, S026, S027, S029, S030, S033, S035, S036, S038, S039). Confidence: high.

6. **Judge a new use of money or time against the best alternative you already have.** "your opportunity cost should govern your own investment decision making." (S029) "If we've got one thing we can do more of, we're not interested in anything that's not better than that." (S033) Sources: 5 (S012, S022, S029, S030, S033). Confidence: high.

7. **Try to destroy your favorite idea before you spend real money on it.** "You just constantly take your own assumptions and try to disprove them." (S004) "I think it's my duty to destroy old ideas." (S030) Sources: 12 (S001, S004, S012, S017, S024, S028, S030, S031, S032, S034, S035, S036). Confidence: high.

8. **Do not hold the opinion until you can state the other side better than its supporters.** "I'm not entitled to have an opinion on this subject unless I can state the arguments against my position better than the people do who are supporting it." (S017) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S017, S028, S036). Confidence: high.

9. **When several forces point the same way, expect an extreme result, not a mild average.** "You get lollapalooza effects when two, three or four forces are all operating in the same direction." (S004) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S012, S018, S025, S027, S030). Confidence: high.

10. **Count a gain only if it stays with you after customers and rivals respond.** "The great lesson in microeconomics is to discriminate between when technology is going to help you and when it's going to kill you." (S003) "It's just that the savings didn't go to you." (S003) Sources: 7 (S003, S012, S026, S028, S034, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

11. **No business is good enough to buy, or to price, at any number.** "No business is so wonderful that you can't overpay for it." (S004) "there's no investment that is so good you can't ruin it by raising the price higher and higher" (S039) Sources: 6 (S003, S004, S026, S036, S038, S039). Confidence: high.

12. **If you must choose, bet on the business, not on a manager who will repair a lousy one.** "averaged out, betting on the quality of a business is better than betting on the quality of management" (S003) "You can't fix these really lousy businesses." (S031) Sources: 5 (S003, S016, S019, S028, S031). Confidence: high.

13. **Keep a margin of safety in cash, debt, and the size of any single loss.** "You want something that will handle some mischances from life and still do well." (S018) "We strive to maintain much liquidity to provide a margin of safety against short-term equity price volatility." (S021) Sources: 10 (S003, S005, S018, S021, S022, S027, S028, S030, S032, S035). Confidence: high.

14. **Follow the consequences of the consequences before you like the first result.** "the consequences have consequences, and the consequences of the consequences have consequences" (S012) Sources: 5 (S003, S008, S012, S025, S031). Confidence: high.

15. **Run the main models as a checklist. Talent does not replace the list.** "And without a checklist, you will screw up time after time, too." (S004) "Checklist routines avoid a lot of errors." (S017) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S012, S017, S028, S031). Confidence: high.

16. **Sell what you would buy yourself, and leave the dirty last dollar.** "sell what you would buy if you were the buyer, not what you can sell by misleading people." (S039) "the safest way to try and get what you want is to try and deserve what you want." (S017) Sources: 10 (S017, S020, S024, S026, S030, S032, S034, S036, S038, S039). Confidence: high.

17. **Throw out a precise number that is not the economics. Prefer roughly right.** "Better roughly right than precisely wrong." (S012) "Better to be roughly right than precisely wrong." (S018) Sources: 8 (S002, S009, S012, S015, S018, S022, S034, S038). Confidence: high.

18. **Trust a system only when the people who decide also bear the consequences.** "the system is responsible in proportion to the degree that the people who make the decisions bear the consequences." (S022) Sources: 5 (S004, S005, S022, S027, S039). Confidence: high.

19. **Hold a few things you understand. Wide diversification is for people who know nothing.** "diversification is a rule for those who don't know anything." (S030) "it's easier to find four things that are above average than it is to find 40." (S039) Sources: 10 (S002, S003, S021, S026, S027, S029, S030, S033, S036, S039). Confidence: high.

20. **Do not expect a structural decline to bounce back, and do not plan to outrun a technology shift you can avoid.** "This decline in physical volume is a serious reverse, not likely to disappear in some bounce back effect." (S019) "We don't solve the problem of technological obsolescence, we avoid it." (S026) Sources: 8 (S016, S019, S021, S023, S026, S028, S031, S035). Confidence: high.

21. **Treat growing bureaucracy as a disease, and do not hire people whose job is to buy things.** "bureaucracy should much more often be treated like the cancers they so much resemble." (S027) "It never had the equivalent of a department of acquisitions under pressure to buy." (S027) Sources: 6 (S003, S027, S031, S032, S033, S039). Confidence: high.

22. **Be reliable. If you are not, the other virtues do not matter.** "If you're unreliable it doesn't matter what your virtues are, you're going to crater immediately." (S017) Sources: 5 (S001, S017, S026, S029, S039). Confidence: high.

23. **When you need someone to move, show them their own interest. A lecture is the weaker tool.** "appealing to interest is likely to work better as a matter of human persuasion than appeal to anything else." (S004) Sources: 3 (S004, S012, S017). Confidence: high.

24. **Arrange life and the balance sheet so a bad year, including a large drop, does not force a stupid act.** "conduct your life so that you can handle the 50% decline with aplomb and grace." (S030) "It's always wise to be prepared for it getting worse." (S028) Sources: 8 (S005, S006, S009, S010, S017, S024, S028, S030). Confidence: high.

25. **Easy money produces wretched behavior. Do not join a fee machine or a boom because everyone else has.** "Large amounts of easy money cause regrettable human behavior. That's Munger's rule." (S029) "When there's enough incentive, bad things will happen." (S031) Sources: 9 (S008, S022, S024, S025, S026, S029, S030, S034, S038). Confidence: high.

26. **Do not forecast the economy to justify a purchase or a pile of cash.** "Berkshire has not made its progress in life by making wonderful macroeconomic predictions." (S018) Sources: 7 (S002, S005, S018, S021, S022, S035, S036). Confidence: high.
