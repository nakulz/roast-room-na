# Positions: Jensen Huang

19 topics from 14 keynotes, 2021-11-09 to 2026-06-01 (S001 to S014). Each stance has a verbatim quote, a source id, and the upload date. Where the line moved, both versions are dated and the later one is marked newer. Topics these talks do not cover are listed at the end.

## Platform
- **Stance:** What you sell is the platform customers can commit a multi-year build to. In 2024 that platform is the generation. In 2026 the company he describes is an infrastructure company. Inferred from S007, S008, S013, S014.
- 2024-03-18: "Blackwell is not a chip. Blackwell is the name of a platform." (S007)
- 2024-06-02: "Every single generation, as you'll see, is not just a GPU, but it's an entire platform." (S008)
- 2026-03-20: "You have to have complete confidence that the trillion dollars you're putting down" (S013) He is talking about use, performance, cost, and a long life.
- 2026-06-01, newer: "NVIDIA has really become an infrastructure company, not just a GPU company" (S014)

- 2026-03-20: "if you have the wrong architecture, even if it's free, it's not cheap enough." (S013)

## Pricing and TCO

- **Over time:**
  - 2021-11-09: "The more you buy, the more you save." (S001) Same line in S003 (2022-09-20), S006 (2023-08-08), and S008 (2024-06-02).
  - 2025-03-25: he says the save line, then updates it in the same talk. "the more you buy the more you make" (S010)
  - 2026-06-01, newer: "The more you buy, the more you make." (S014)
- 2023-03-21: "Datacenter operators optimize for throughput and total cost of ownership of the entire datacenter." (S004)
- 2023-03-21: "H100 can reduce large language model processing costs by an order of magnitude." (S004)
- 2023-08-08: "so 20 times less power and 12 times less cost." (S006) Same budget, accelerated versus unaccelerated.
- 2025-03-25: "the smarter the AI the less you can make in volume very sensible tradeoff" (S010)
- 2025-10-28: "On the one hand, GB200 is the most expensive computer. On the other hand, its token generation capability is so great that it produces it at the lowest cost." (S012)
- 2025-10-28: "It is the lowest cost way to generate tokens." (S012)
- 2026-03-20: "The longer you could use it, the lower the cost." (S013)

## Energy and power
- **Stance:** Power caps the factory, so revenue is tokens per watt. Accelerate every workload you can, put the rest on CPUs, and spend the saved watts on compute. Inferred from S004, S007, S008, S010, S012, S013, S014.
- 2023-03-21: "First and foremost, data centers must accelerate every workload." (S004)
- 2023-03-21: "Acceleration will reclaim power. The energy saved can fuel new growth. Whatever is not accelerated will be processed on CPUs." (S004)
- 2024-03-18: "Our goal is to continuously drive down the cost and the energy." (S007)
- 2024-06-02: "Our token generation performance has made it possible for us to drive the Energy down by 45000x." (S008)
- 2025-03-25: "every single data center in the future will be power limited your revenues are power limited" (S010)
- 2025-03-25: "energy is our most important commodity everything is related ultimate to energy" (S010)
- 2025-10-28: "we need energy to win." (S012)
- 2026-03-20: "A one gigawatt factory will never become two." (S013)
- 2026-03-20: "every data center, every single factory, by definition, is power constrained." (S013)
- 2026-06-01, newer: "Performance per watt is your revenues." (S014)
- 2026-06-01: "Today's AI factories over-provision power by up to 40%." (S014)

## Inference
- **Stance:** Inference is the hard, revenue-bearing workload. He has called it that since 2021. By 2025 he says thinking made the compute need about 100 times what he had thought a year earlier, and that the world was wrong to treat inference as easy. Inferred from S001, S007, S010, S012, S013.
- 2021-11-09: "Inferencing is arguably one of the most technically challenging runtime engines the world has ever seen." (S001)
- 2024-03-18: "Some people call it inference, but it's more appropriately generation." (S007)

## Metrics
- **Stance:** Once the data center is a factory, the numbers that matter are throughput, tokens per second per user, utilization, and tokens per watt. Those are the revenue. Inferred from S007, S008, S010, S011, S013, S014.
- 2024-03-18: "So your throughput dictates the cost of delivering the service." (S007)
- 2024-06-02: "Your throughput directly correlates to your revenues" (S008)
- 2025-03-25: "your goal is to maximize the area under the curve" (S010)
- 2025-03-25: "the way you think about a very easy gut feel gut check on whether your AI factories are making progress is Watts divided by those numbers" (S010)
- 2025-06-11: "If the utilization of that factory is not at its fullest, the cost to the factory owner is going to be incredible." (S011)
- 2026-03-20: "you will be studying the throughput and the token speed of your AI factories" (S013)
- 2026-06-01: "Compute is revenue now. Compute is profit." (S014)

## Digital twins
- **Stance:** Anything physical that software will run at scale gets built, tested, and operated as a twin first. The same rule covers the robot and the AI factory. Inferred from S003, S007, S010, S011, S014.
- 2022-09-20: "No physical product operated by software can be deployed in large numbers without testing the software on its digital twin." (S003)
- 2024-03-18: "How else would you test the software?" (S007)
- 2025-03-25: "before you build a data center you have to build a digital twin" (S010)
- 2025-06-11: "Everything physical will be built digitally." (S011)
- 2026-06-01: "before a single rack lands" (S014) Design and validate the factory in the twin first.
